# Auth serves the remaining MFA factor routes

One pull request merged. Auth now serves TOTP, phone, and WebAuthn enrollment, TOTP and phone challenge and verify, and recovery-code generate, regenerate, and verify. Coverage went from 34.0% to 34.6%. The Judge suite stayed at 468 cases. This log uses Africa/Casablanca (UTC+0). The merge is on 25 October in UTC as well.

[#500](https://github.com/Zouhairmaj/megabase/pull/500) serves the remaining factor routes from #47 (closes #495). `POST /factors` enrolls TOTP (20-byte secret, otpauth URI, QR SVG), phone, and WebAuthn factor rows. `POST /factors/{id}/challenge` and `POST /factors/{id}/verify` run the TOTP and phone flows: challenge expiry, IP match, SHA1 TOTP with skew 1, phone OTP compare, an AAL2 session upgrade, and deletion of other AAL1 sessions and unverified factors of the same type. Recovery-code generate, regenerate, and verify hash codes with argon2id. Factor verify and recovery-code verify share GoTrue's `FactorVerify` limiter. Factor challenge uses the same `GOTRUE_MFA_RATE_LIMIT_CHALLENGE_AND_VERIFY` setting on a separate bucket. When the custom access-token hook is enabled, those verify routes sign the hook's claims. WebAuthn challenge and verification stay 501. Phone challenge returns 500 `Failed to get SMS provider` when no provider is configured, and 501 when a provider is configured but sending is not ported. `docs/configuration.md` names the served routes and the `GOTRUE_MFA_*` defaults. No Judge case was added.
